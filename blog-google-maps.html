Local SEO

How Google Maps brings customers to your door

A plain-language guide to setting up your Google Business Profile properly.

When someone searches "near me," Google doesn't guess — it looks at your Google Business Profile to decide who shows up on the map and in local results. Getting this right is one of the highest-value things a small shop can do online, and it costs nothing but a bit of time.

Start with your Google Business Profile

This is the free listing that shows your shop on Google Maps and in search results, complete with hours, photos, reviews, and directions. If you haven't claimed and verified yours yet, this is the first step — everything else builds on it.

Get the basics exactly right

  • Name, address, and phone number should match exactly what's on your shop signage and website. Inconsistent details confuse Google and can hurt your ranking.
  • Category matters more than people think — pick the most specific one that fits (e.g. "Tailor" rather than just "Clothing store").
  • Hours should be kept current, especially around holidays. Nothing loses trust faster than a customer showing up to a "closed" shop that was listed as open.

Photos do more work than you'd expect

Listings with real, recent photos get significantly more attention than ones with none. A few clear shots of your storefront, your products, and the inside of your shop help people recognize you the moment they arrive — and signal that the listing is actually active.

Reviews build the trust Google rewards

Ask happy customers to leave a quick review. You don't need hundreds — a steady trickle of genuine, recent reviews tells both Google and future customers that people keep coming back.

Connect your website to your listing

Once your profile is solid, linking it to a proper website closes the loop: someone finds you on Maps, taps through, and lands on a page that answers their next question — what you sell, what it costs, and how to reach you.

Local SEO isn't a trick. It's just making sure Google has accurate information to give the people already looking for you.
